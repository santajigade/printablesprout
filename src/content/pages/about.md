PrintableSprout is a small site that makes free printables for parents and teachers: coloring pages, chore and reward charts, daily routine charts, holiday activities and simple games.

## Why this site exists

Most free printables online are scans of the same few pages, files hidden behind email sign-ups, or pages that print with half the picture cut off. We wanted the opposite: original line art, a page that prints right the first time on both US Letter and A4, and no sign-up wall.

Every page also works on screen. Kids can color with tap-to-fill, a watercolor brush or a crayon tool on a phone, tablet or computer, and parents can type a child's name onto the sheet before printing.

## Who makes it

PrintableSprout is run by [Santaji Gadepatil](/author/santaji-gadepatil/), an Electronics and Telecommunication engineer from Pune, India, who designs and tests every printable. Pages are drafted with the help of AI tools, then checked by hand: each sheet is test printed, each fact is checked against an official source, and anything we cannot verify is left out. Read the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for the full process.

## What you can do with our printables

Print as many copies as your family or classroom needs. Please share a link to the page instead of the file, and do not sell the printables or upload them to other sites. See the [terms of use](/terms-of-use/).
