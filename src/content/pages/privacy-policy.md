## Who we are

PrintableSprout (printablesprout.com) offers free printables. Questions about this policy can be sent to [contact@printablesprout.com](mailto:contact@printablesprout.com).

## The coloring tool and names

The online coloring tool runs entirely in your browser. Names you type onto a sheet, the colors you choose and the pictures you download are not sent to us or stored on our servers.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. If you email us, we keep your message and email address only to reply. Like most websites, our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

## Learning games

Our learning games pages (printablesprout.com/games/) are made for children. They load no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and they show no personalized ads. Children never sign up, and the games do not ask for or store any personal information.

## Parent Premium notify list

A parent, guardian or teacher aged 18 or over can leave an email address to hear when Parent Premium opens. We store only that email and the date, use it only for that one announcement, and never sell or share it. Email us to be removed at any time.

## Analytics and advertising

The rest of the site works as follows.

We may use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful, and Google AdSense to show ads that pay for hosting. These services may use cookies. You can learn how Google uses data at [Google's privacy and terms page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can manage personalized ads at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Children

This site is designed for parents and teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Contact us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
