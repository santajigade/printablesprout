This page explains how every printable on PrintableSprout is made and checked.

## Original art only

Every sheet is drawn for this site as simple line art. We never use famous characters, brand mascots, logos or artwork copied from books, films, games or other websites. If a request would need a protected character, we make an original design instead.

## How pages are written

Each printable page gives practical help: who it suits, how to use it, how to adjust it for different ages and how to print it well. Drafts are prepared with the help of AI tools and then reviewed and edited by our designer, [Santaji Gadepatil](/author/santaji-gadepatil/), before publishing.

## How facts are checked

Any fact on a page, such as a holiday date or a tip from a health organization, must come from a reliable source read while preparing that page. We prefer official sources such as U.S. government sites (.gov), the American Academy of Pediatrics, the CDC and major museums. If a fact cannot be verified, it is left out. We do not invent quotes, statistics or personal stories.

## Testing before publishing

Every printable is checked on US Letter and A4 paper sizes, on a phone and on a computer, and with the online coloring tool, before it goes live.

## Health and safety topics

Charts about routines, sleep or chores share general tips only. They are not medical or professional advice. When a page mentions health guidance, it links to the official source so you can read it in full.

## Updates and corrections

Seasonal pages are reviewed before each season and updated when dates or details change. The updated date at the top of each page shows the last review. See the [corrections policy](/corrections-policy/) to report an error.

## Advertising

PrintableSprout may show ads to cover hosting costs. Ads never decide what we publish, and we do not accept payment to feature products inside printables.
