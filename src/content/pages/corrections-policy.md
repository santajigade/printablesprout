We want every printable and every fact on this site to be right.

## How to report an error

Send the page link and what looks wrong through our [contact page](/contact/). Printing problems count too: if a sheet cuts off on your printer or paper size, tell us your paper size and browser.

## What we do next

We review every report. If a fact is wrong, we fix it and update the date at the top of the page. If a change affects how a printable is used, such as a wrong date on a calendar or countdown, we add a short note on the page saying what changed and when.

## What we do not change

We do not rewrite pages to remove fair criticism, and we do not hide corrections.
