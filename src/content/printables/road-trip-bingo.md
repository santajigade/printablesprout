Road trip bingo turns the view out the car window into a game. Each card has 16 picture squares, from a cow and a red barn to a stop sign, a bridge and a water tower. When your child spots one, they color that square. Four colored squares in a row, across, down or corner to corner, is BINGO. Pick **Road card A** and **Road card B** for two kids in the back seat (same pictures, different spots, so nobody wins at the same moment), or print the **Holiday lights card** for trips to see family in December.

## Why this sheet is ready for this week

Holiday traffic is real. A <a href="https://www.foxweather.com/lifestyle/more-than-81-million-americans-expected-travel-thanksgiving-despite-fewer-drivers-flyers" target="_blank" rel="noopener">FOX Weather report on the AAA forecast</a> says about 81.5 million Americans were expected to travel at least 50 miles from home between Tuesday, November 24 and Monday, November 30, 2026, and about 72.9 million of them by car. That is a lot of back seat time, and a game with pictures keeps young passengers busy without a screen.

## Three cards and how to choose

| Card | What is on it | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Road card A | Car, truck, bus, stop sign, traffic light, airplane, cow, bridge, gas pump, tree, house, water tower, train, bird, barn, bike | Highways and country roads |
| Road card B | The same 16 pictures in a new order | A second player, so two kids can race |
| Holiday lights card | Snowman, wreath, house lights, holiday tree, car with a tree on top, snowplow, sled, candy cane and more | December drives and evening light tours |

Type a name before printing and the title becomes "Emma's Road Trip Bingo." For the car, slide each sheet into a clipboard or onto a hardcover book and bring crayons in a zip bag. Colored pencils break less often when the car bumps.

## Play it three ways

**Classic bingo.** Spot it, call it out, color it. The first player with four in a row says BINGO and picks the next snack or song.

**Star collector.** Keep playing after the first bingo. The bottom strip has 10 stars because a 4 by 4 card has 10 possible lines. Color a star for each line. Older kids can try to fill all 10 stars before you arrive.

**Blackout challenge.** The hardest mode: color all 16 squares before the end of the trip. On long drives, save it for the last hour, when everyone needs a new goal.

## Rules that stop back seat arguments

1. Agree before you start that a picture counts only when the player who spots it says it out loud.
2. A sight can be claimed by both players. Siblings on cards A and B can both color the cow.
3. The driver never plays. A grown up in the passenger seat can be the referee.
4. Tie? Whoever has more stars colored wins the round.

## A quick car seat check before the game starts

Games work best when everyone is buckled in the right seat. <a href="https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/car-seats-and-booster-seats" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NHTSA's car seat guide</a> says to keep children in the back seat at least through age 12, and to keep a child in a booster seat until the seat belt fits properly. The <a href="https://www.cdc.gov/child-passenger-safety" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CDC child passenger safety page</a> adds that children should be properly buckled on every trip. A tip for bingo: keep the sheet on a lap board, not held up high, and stop the game if a child unbuckles to see out the other window.
