The kids' table at Thanksgiving, which lands on Thursday, November 26 this year according to the <a href="https://www.wiwb.uscourts.gov/node/28" target="_blank" rel="noopener">2026 federal holiday schedule</a>, often has a long wait between sitting down and eating. This one page placemat keeps children busy with four short activities, each in its own box, so a child can stop after one and still feel finished.

## What is on the placemat

- **Help the turkey reach the pie:** a maze with a single correct path, sized for crayons.
- **Pumpkin tic-tac-toe:** two grids, so siblings can play a rematch without a second sheet.
- **Draw your favorite food:** an empty plate with a fork and knife, ready for drawing.
- **I am thankful for:** four numbered lines for a short gratitude list.

Type a name in the box above the sheet and it prints as "Leo's Thanksgiving Placemat", which helps with place settings when you have many kids at one table.

## Setting up a calm kids' table

The value of a placemat is less about the sheet and more about the setup. A few things make it work better:

1. **Print one per child plus two spares.** Spills happen, and a fresh sheet ends tears fast.
2. **Put out one cup of crayons per two children.** Sharing a small cup is easier than passing a big box.
3. **Tape each sheet down at two corners.** It stops sliding when plates arrive.
4. **Start with the maze.** It is the quietest activity and buys the most time.
5. **Save the thankful list for dessert.** Children can read their list out loud while adults finish eating.

## Which box suits which age

| Age | Best boxes | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 4 to 5 | Plate drawing, tic-tac-toe | Play tic-tac-toe with them first, then let them play each other |
| 6 to 7 | Maze, plate, thankful list | They can write one word on each thankful line |
| 8 to 9 | All four | Ask them to time themselves on the maze |

Simple games like this also suit the kind of everyday play the <a href="https://www.cdc.gov/child-development/positive-parenting-tips/preschooler-3-5-years.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CDC's positive parenting tips for preschoolers</a> encourage, such as reading together and letting young children help with small jobs.

## Playing on a tablet instead

On a phone or tablet, choose Crayon mode to trace the maze path with a finger, and use the X and O spaces for tic-tac-toe. Tap to fill colors the plate, the boxes and the pie. Press **Undo** to take back a move.

## After dinner

Collect the placemats and keep one from each child as a small Thanksgiving keepsake.
